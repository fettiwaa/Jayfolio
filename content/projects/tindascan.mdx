## Overview

TindaScan is a mobile inventory and sales system for small sari-sari stores. The cashier scans a product's barcode, builds a cart, takes cash, and the sale and stock update are saved together. The owner sees daily sales, best sellers, and low-stock alerts, and exports reports to Excel.

It is our final project for CS 413 (Advanced Software Engineering) at Batangas State University, built as a team of two with **Cloyd Robin Castillo**. I worked on it as a co-developer.

## Problem

Most sari-sari stores track stock and sales in a notebook, or not at all. Owners find out an item is out of stock when a customer asks for it, cannot tell which products actually make money, and have no record of what a helper sold while they were away. Connectivity in many stores is also unreliable, so a tool that needs constant internet is not usable.

## Solution

An Android-first Flutter app on Firebase, with no custom server:

1. **Scan and sell.** The phone camera reads standard retail barcodes (EAN-13, the format on most Philippine products) and adds the item to the cart.
2. **Atomic checkout.** Each sale is one Firestore transaction: it re-reads stock and prices, creates the receipt, lowers stock, logs the stock movement, and updates the day's totals. If any item is short, nothing is saved.
3. **Two roles.** The owner manages products, staff, reports, and settings. Staff can only sell. The app hides screens by role, and Firestore security rules enforce it on the server.
4. **Offline selling.** Without internet, the sale is saved on the phone and synced later. Offline receipts are marked, and any stock that ends up negative after syncing is flagged to the owner.

## Key Features

- **Barcode checkout** — Continuous scanning, quantity steppers, swipe to remove with undo, and quick-cash buttons for the amount tendered
- **No double charges** — A sale ID is created with the cart and reused on retry, so a lost connection cannot record the same sale twice
- **Owner dashboard** — Today's sales, profit estimate, 7- and 30-day trends, best sellers, and lowest stock
- **Inventory** — Restocks, spoilage, and adjustments with a full movement history, plus low-stock, out-of-stock, and expiry alerts
- **Reports** — Sales, best sellers, inventory, and stock movements, exported as `.xlsx`
- **Staff accounts** — The owner creates and deactivates staff; a deactivated account is signed out immediately
- **English and Filipino**, light and dark themes

## System Architecture

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Presentation (Flutter)                       │
│  Sell / Checkout · BarcodeScannerView         │
│  go_router role guard                         │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Business logic (Riverpod)                    │
│  CartNotifier · CheckoutService · AlertService│
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Data (repository interfaces)                 │
│  ProductRepository · SalesRepository · ...    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Backend as a Service (Firebase)              │
│  Authentication · Cloud Firestore + rules     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Money is stored as whole centavos to avoid rounding errors, and every repository returns typed failures (`InsufficientStock`, `ProductNotFound`, `NetworkUnavailable`, and others) so the screens never handle raw Firebase errors.

## Security

The app's role guard only controls what each user sees. The real enforcement is in Firestore security rules: only active users can write, a sale must belong to the cashier who made it, change must equal tendered minus total, sales cannot be edited or deleted, and stock can only go down as part of a new sale. Dashboard totals must add up to exactly the recorded sales.
